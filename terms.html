Website Terms.

A practical starter terms page for buyers to customize to their entity, services, website functionality, and governing law.

Template note

This is illustrative template copy, not legal advice. Have qualified counsel adapt it before publication.

Use of this website

Set out the conditions under which visitors may access and use the website, including any prohibited use, interference, scraping, or unlawful activity.

Informational content

Clarify that general website content does not create a professional advisory relationship and should not be treated as individualized financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.

Intellectual property

Describe ownership and permitted use of the brand, website design, written content, graphics, and other materials published on the site.

Third-party services

Explain that links, embeds, scheduling tools, analytics, and other third-party services may operate under their own terms and policies.

Disclaimers and liability

Include limitations appropriate to your jurisdiction and business model, avoiding promises that the website will always be uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for every purpose.

Changes and contact

State how updates to these terms will be communicated and provide the correct legal business contact before publishing.

FAQ

Questions before the first conversation.

Straight answers about who NOVEO is for, how engagements work, what we need from your team, and what a fractional CFO relationship actually looks like.

If your question is specific to your business model, stage, or finance setup, the fastest route is a short discovery conversation.

Fit & scope

Is NOVEO the right kind of finance support?

Start here if you are deciding whether you need project support, recurring advisory, or a fractional CFO.

What kind of companies does NOVEO work best with?
Founder-led and growth-stage businesses that have outgrown bookkeeping or basic monthly reporting, but do not necessarily need a full-time CFO. Typical fits include SaaS, agencies, professional services, e-commerce, and multi-model businesses.
How do I know if I need a fractional CFO?
A common signal is that leadership is making bigger hiring, pricing, capital, margin, or growth decisions faster than the current finance setup can support. If the business has accurate books but still lacks a dependable forward view, fractional CFO support can be useful.
Can we start with a smaller project?
Yes. Many engagements start with a focused need such as rebuilding a forecast, improving management reporting, preparing for capital, or understanding margin. Ongoing support can be added later if the business needs it.
Do you replace our bookkeeper or accountant?
Usually no. NOVEO works above and alongside bookkeeping, tax, and accounting support. We rely on accurate historical data, then add planning, reporting, decision support, and financial leadership around it.
Working together

What does the engagement actually look like?

The cadence and scope depend on the business, but the working principles stay consistent.

How often do we meet?
Focused projects may have milestone-based sessions. Ongoing advisory can be monthly or biweekly. Fractional CFO relationships are often weekly or tied to the leadership operating cadence. The right frequency depends on decision volume and complexity.
What do you need from our team?
Access to current financials, relevant operating data, context on the business model, and timely input on assumptions. We also need a clear owner on your side who can help keep data and decisions moving.
What systems do you work with?
The exact stack varies, but the work typically connects accounting data, spreadsheets, CRM or revenue data, payroll, and relevant operating systems. The goal is not to replace every tool; it is to make the financial view coherent and usable.
How quickly can we start?
Timing depends on current capacity and the complexity of your setup. After discovery, we define scope, required inputs, and a realistic start plan before committing to dates.
Pricing & outcomes

What should buyers expect commercially?

Finance advisory should be clear about scope without making promises a responsible firm cannot guarantee.

How is pricing structured?
Pricing follows scope, complexity, cadence, and senior involvement. NOVEO supports focused projects, ongoing advisory, and fractional CFO engagements. We define the commercial model after discovery rather than forcing every company into a standard tier.
Do you guarantee specific financial results?
No. Better forecasting and finance leadership can improve decision quality and visibility, but financial outcomes depend on the business, market, execution, and many factors outside an advisor’s control. Case-study metrics are examples, not guarantees.
Can the scope change as we grow?
Yes. Ongoing engagements should stay right-sized. Support can expand into capital, board reporting, finance operations, or deeper planning as complexity increases, and it can be reduced when the need changes.

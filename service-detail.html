What this solves

Finance is too important to stay reactive.

A growing business can have accurate books and still make poor financial decisions. The gap usually appears between historical reporting and what leadership needs to decide next.

Your CFO should help the business see around corners.

NOVEO builds the forward-looking layer: forecasts, scenarios, management reporting, decision models, and senior financial guidance tied to the operating plan.

The result is a clearer answer to questions like when to hire, how fast to grow, what margins can support, whether to raise capital, and where cash risk is building.

Good fit

You probably need this when...

These are common signals that the business has moved beyond basic finance support.

Signal 01

Decisions are moving faster than reporting.

Leadership is committing to hires, spend, or growth before finance can show the impact.

Signal 02

The forecast is not trusted.

Cash, hiring, and revenue plans live in separate spreadsheets or depend on optimistic assumptions.

Signal 03

The founder is still the finance translator.

Too much time is spent explaining numbers, preparing investor updates, or rebuilding context manually.

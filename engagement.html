What affects scope

Pricing follows complexity, not a marketing tier.

These are the factors we use to shape an engagement before work begins.

Business complexity

Revenue model and operating structure.

Multiple entities, business models, geographies, or revenue streams require more finance architecture than a simple single-model business.

Data readiness

How clean and connected the inputs are.

Existing reporting, chart-of-accounts quality, CRM data, payroll, and operational systems affect setup effort.

Cadence

How often leadership needs finance in the room.

Monthly advisory, weekly reviews, and embedded CFO participation create different levels of ongoing involvement.

Decision scope

What finance needs to help decide.

Capital, board reporting, pricing, M&A, expansion, or high-growth hiring generally require more senior support.

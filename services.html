When to bring us in

The signal is usually a decision you cannot price confidently.

Clients typically reach out when growth creates financial questions that bookkeeping alone cannot answer.

Hiring

Can we afford the next team?

Model the cash, margin, capacity, and timing impact before increasing fixed cost.

Runway

How much time do we really have?

Replace optimistic cash assumptions with a scenario-based runway view leadership can use.

Margins

Where is growth actually profitable?

Understand contribution by customer, service, channel, or product before scaling the wrong thing.

Capital

Should we raise, borrow, or stay lean?

Compare financing options against the operating plan instead of choosing capital in isolation.
